Privacy Policy

Prescription Vault · Last updated 12 September 2026

Prescription Vault does not collect, transmit, or store any of your data outside your own device. There are no accounts, no servers involved in normal use, no analytics, and no advertising.

What the app stores

The app stores the records you create: the people you add, the visits you record, the medicines you enter, and the photographs or documents you attach. All of it is written to a database and a folder inside the app's private storage on your phone.

This information never leaves your device unless you choose to share it.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-up, email address, or phone number is required.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or usage tracking.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers are read.
  • No third-party services receive your data.
  • Your records are not uploaded to any server.

Permissions

  • Camera — used only when you choose to photograph a prescription. Photographs are saved into the app and nowhere else.
  • Photos and media — used only when you choose to attach an existing image or document.

Both permissions are requested at the moment you use the corresponding feature, and the app works without them if you enter records by hand.

Location data in photographs

Photographs taken on a phone often carry hidden metadata, including the GPS coordinates of where the picture was taken. For a prescription photo that would identify the clinic you visited.

Prescription Vault re-encodes every image it stores, which discards that metadata. The copy kept on your device contains the picture and nothing else.

Sharing

The app can export a visit as a PDF and pass it to another app you choose — a messaging app, email, or cloud storage. This happens only when you tap share and select a destination. Once the file leaves the app, it is governed by the privacy policy of whichever app you sent it to.

Deleting your data

Deleting a visit or a page removes both the record and the stored image from your device. Uninstalling the app removes everything it holds. There is no copy anywhere else, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf and no request you need to make.

Children

The app is not directed at children. Because no data is collected or transmitted, no information about any user, of any age, is received.

Legal basis and your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, obligations attach to the processing of personal data by a data fiduciary. Prescription Vault does not act as a fiduciary over your records: no personal data is collected, received, or processed by the developer or by any third party. The data stays under your sole control on your own device.

Your rights of access, correction, and erasure are exercised directly in the app, which can edit and delete any record it holds.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app introduces optional cloud backup, that feature will require you to create an account and opt in explicitly, and this policy will be updated before it ships. Until then, the app has no network features.
